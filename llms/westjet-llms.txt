# Westjet: WestJet has pioneered low-cost travel in Canada, cutting airfares in half, and increasing the flying population in Canada by more than 50 per cent. Today, more than 15,000 WestJetters support our nearly 200 aircraft, connecting guests globally to over 100 destinations across North America, Central America and the Caribbean, Europe and Asia.

> WestJet is a Canadian airline headquartered in Calgary, Alberta, offering scheduled flights across Canada, the United States, Mexico, the Caribbean, Central America, Asia and Europe. WestJet serves over 100 destinations with a fleet of Boeing 737 and 787 Dreamliner aircraft. The airline provides economy, premium economy, and business class cabins, along with a loyalty program (WestJet Rewards) and vacation packages.

WestJet operates westjet.com as its primary digital platform for booking flights, managing reservations, checking in, and accessing travel information. The site is bilingual (English and French) and serves travellers across multiple regions.
